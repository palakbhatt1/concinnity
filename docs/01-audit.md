# 01 — Audit of the current homepage

Based on the 11 screenshots of solopreneuros.io (desktop) plus the page text.

## What works (keep)
- "Why this exists" rows: pain struck through → fix with an arrow. Clear and scannable.
- Mess-to-dashboard metaphor. The founder already likes it.
- Module chips with color-coded icons.
- Consistent orange accent, clean type.
- 3-step access timeline.

## Priority for the presentation
Lead with **1, 3, 8, 10, 12, 15, 18**. The rest are supporting.

## One-line diagnosis
The page tells visitors what modules exist but never shows the product at work, and the best idea on it (mess → dashboard) is delayed, mislabeled, and silent about being interactive.
